# v0.1 Intercept-20 smoke results

This is a one-seed, 100-transition plumbing check for the authored-planted tier. It is not a comparative estimate and is far below the budget at which swarm allocation or InkCheck's mature portfolio should be judged.

Configuration:

- story: `the-intercept-20` (`intercept-diverse-20-v1`);
- 20 replay-verified planted defects, 19 fault types, 16 source locations;
- algorithms: random, systematic, simple coverage, minimal swarm;
- search seed/runtime seed: 1/1;
- budget: 100 choice transitions;
- all four cells completed; no resource stop.

| Algorithm | Distinct bugs | Fraction | First discovery | Wall ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| random | 5/20 | 25% | 11 | 78.6 |
| systematic | 4/20 | 20% | 6 | 58.9 |
| coverage | 8/20 | 40% | 6 | 50.1 |
| swarm | 6/20 | 30% | 2 | 39.8 |

The paired union was 9/20 bugs. One bug was swarm-only in this single cell; 11 were missed by all four. This is useful evidence that the multi-bug and complementarity accounting works, not evidence that swarm has a stable exclusive capability. Repeated logarithmic-budget experiments—including the real InkCheck adapter—are required before making that claim.

Separate reachability validation replayed an exact witness for every one of the 20 oracles. Search-generated spot checks also found 19/20 in a 5,000-transition random run (seed 2), while a deterministic 5,000-transition DFS found the deep escape/revisit subset. These are reachability and calibration observations, not estimates of expected performance.

## One-cell 5,000-unit adapter probe

A second one-seed probe included the real InkCheck v0.7.2 adapter at 5,000 native work units. In-process algorithms use choice transitions; InkCheck uses states explored, so the budget columns are adjacent but not identical work. Every cell completed under a 1,536 MiB heap watermark and 60-second guard.

| Algorithm | Native unit | Distinct bugs | Fraction | First discovery | Wall ms |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| random | choice transitions | 17/20 | 85% | 11 | 1,853 |
| systematic | choice transitions | 10/20 | 50% | 6 | 2,521 |
| coverage | choice transitions | 20/20 | 100% | 6 | 3,439 |
| swarm | choice transitions | 20/20 | 100% | 2 | 1,773 |
| InkCheck | states explored | 15/20 | 75% | 67 | 1,362 |

The union was all 20 bugs, with no exclusive discovery in this one cell. Coverage and swarm both saturated this particular mutation set at the grant, while random, deterministic DFS, and InkCheck found different subsets. That is a useful calibration result, but it does not rank the methods: it has one seed, mixed native work units, no confidence intervals, and a fixture used during implementation. The preregistered repeated budget ladder remains the comparative test.
